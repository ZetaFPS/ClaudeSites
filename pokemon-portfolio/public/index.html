Pre-grade a card

Upload the front and back to estimate centering, corners, edges and surface.

Search and select the exact card. Its official picture is compared with your photo, so the card’s own artwork and printed lines aren’t mistaken for creases or marks — and you’ll see its value at the estimated grade.

For the best estimate
  • Lay the card out of its sleeve on a plain surface that contrasts with its border — mid-grey or wood works for both sides (the back’s border is dark blue).
  • Shoot straight down so the card fills most of the photo.
  • Use even, indirect light — no glare or shadows across the card.
  • Keep it in focus; edges and corners need to be sharp.

Your grade report appears here

Centering is measured in millimetres from your photos and checked against PSA’s centering standards. Edges, corners and surface are scanned for whitening, dings, creases and marks.
